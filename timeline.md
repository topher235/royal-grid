## MVP Features

- Player can play the default game mode with effects
- Player can stop whenever and then continue playing on the next start-up (save system)
- Player can choose 1 other map and non-default character
- The game's art and UI are more-or-less fully designed
- Good sound design and animations
- Player is presented with a carousel of illustrations and context about the game's rules on first load
- User has an option to find the tutorial carousel somewhere in the main menu or submenu
- Statistics are tracked

- Ads are implemented or at least started
